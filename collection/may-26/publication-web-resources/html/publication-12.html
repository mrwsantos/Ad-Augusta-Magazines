Tennis

Term 1 has been a very busy and successful term for the Premier Tennis team, starting off with a 16-5 win during the traditional exchange with Christchurch Boys’ High School.

The annual Quadrangular Tournament involved teams from Hamilton Boys’ High School, Palmerston North Boys’ High School, and Wellington College. After wins against Hamilton Boys’ High School and Palmerston North Boys’ High School, the Premier Tennis team won the exchange for the first time in seven years, with a 6-0 win against last year’s winners Wellington College.

The Auckland Champion of Champions’ Tournament included the top two Junior, Intermediate and Senior players from all Auckland secondary schools. Captain of Tennis Luca Bland won the senior singles title, and he teamed up with Ridley Huang to win the senior doubles title. Charles Kindt and Yida Wu also made it into the Intermediate doubles final but unfortunately had to withdraw.

In the Auckland Premier competition, the Premier team (Campbell Dunwoodie, Ridley Huang, Austen Keni, Charles Kindt, Jiachen Li, Yida Wu and Captain Luca Bland) finished second in the pool. After a win over St Kentigern College in the quarter-final and Westlake Boys’ High School in the semi-final, the team played King’s College and claimed the Auckland title with a 5-1 win (below).

sport

The Premier Water Polo team finished the Auckland League in a credible fifth place. The team did start the 2026 season on the back foot, with two heavy losses to Sacred Heart College and Westlake Boys’ High School.

However, the team showed tremendous perseverance and showcased some exceptional Water Polo throughout the remainder of the season. Barring a loss to a tenacious side from Rosmini College, the team would have made the top four of the tournament, after winning all their other games.

The Premier team performed well in the final for fifth and sixth place, controlling the game from the start and defeating Rangitoto College 9-7.

The other notable event of the season was the annual traditional exchange with Christchurch Boys’ High School in February. Auckland Grammar School hosted a five-team tournament with teams from Westlake Boys’ High School, Hamilton Boys’ High School and Tauranga Boys’ College making up the competition. Some great Water Polo was played, and the friendships created by billeting players from other schools were appreciated by all.

Two Auckland Grammar School teams competed inthe Interschool Touch Rugby competitions in Term 1.

The Senior team played in the Central Eastern Zone with a mixture of results. However, it gave many students their first opportunity to play Touch Rugbyin a competitive environment and improve their skills and knowledge of the game.

Many players were returning for their third and fourth years in the Premier Touch Rugby team. This proved invaluable with the Premier team finishing second in pool play behind King’s College. The team took on Kelston Boys’ High School in the semi-final, coming from behind to secure a spot in the final with abuzzer-beating try. Facing King’s College in the final, both teams showed incredible resilience, going try for try to have the scores locked at 7-7. However, a late, final try to King’s College saw the Premier team become runners-up for the 2026 season.

athletics

A group of 208 athletes competed at the School’s Athletics Sports Day in February. In optimal summer conditions at Go Media Stadium Mt Smart, athletes gave it their utmost best, putting their athletic fortitude to the test.

The 2026 Junior Champion was Form 3 student Bruno Murdoch. Bruno took home Silver in both the 300m and 800m races, before winning Gold in the three Jump events - Long Jump, High Jump and Triple Jump. Jia Wen Lu claimed four medals across the day - Gold in the 200m, Silver in both the 80m Hurdles and the 100m, and Bronze in the Long Jump.

Form 6 student Iulai Masoe was named as the Intermediate Champion for 2026, winning Gold for the 200m and 400m races, and Silver for the 100m hurdles, the Long Jump and High Jump. A new School record was set by Form 6 student Leyon Caffery who won the Discus with a distance of 58.08m, improving the record setby Samiuela Ulufonua 14 years ago by 2.03m.

The Senior competition did not disappoint, portraying top athletic form across all events. Form 6 student Harrison Day set a new record in the Senior 400m, beating 2025 Deputy Head Prefect Charles Howlett’s record by just one hundredth of a second. The longer track races - the 1500m and the 3000m - were both won by Form 6 student Ethan Mora, with Isaac Stewart picking up the Silver.

Captain of Athletics Luke Thomson was able to run his first time below 11 seconds when he came first in the 100m, winning in a time of 10.96s. He then went on to win the Gold medal in the 200m and Silver medals in the 400m and the Long Jump. These results enabled him tobe crowned the Senior Champion for 2026.

At the Auckland Athletics Championships held across two days in March, almost 100 students represented Auckland Grammar School, earning a total of 34 medals (12 Gold, 13 Silver and nine Bronze). In addition, the Senior 4 x 100m Relay team of Max Browne, Harrison Day, Lincoln Evans and Luke Thomson won the Gold medal in a New Record time of 42.99 seconds.

These achievements would not have been possible without the help of Stuart Blackwell, Marshall Hall, Chris Oates, Briana Stephenson, Stephen Thorpe, Matt Wyatt and Master in Charge Tienie van Wyk, to whom the School’s Athletics team extends their heartfelt thanks.

cricket

After winning the 2025 Premier One-Day final over Westlake Boys’ High School to claim the Auckland title for the first time since 2000, the 1st XI Cricket team earneda place at the New Zealand Secondary School Boys’ 1st XI Cup finals. The team finished first equal on points and recorded a win over eventual champions Wellington College. However, inclement weather that negatively impacted the net run rate saw the team finish third overall.

The largely new squad assembled in January, with only three returning players alongside several younger athletes. The team demonstrated significant development throughout a demanding Term 1 schedule. Valuable lessons were gained during the first three-day fixture against New Plymouth Boys’ High School, highlighting the requirements for success in longer-format cricket. These insights were effectively applied in the subsequent home fixture against Christchurch Boys’ High School, resulting in the team’s first victory in the red-ball format. Further growth was evident in the final traditional match against Palmerston North Boys’ High School.

In the Auckland One-Day competition, the team showed resilience and consistency. Highlights included a one-run victory over King’s College and two successful 200-plus run chases against Macleans College and Mount Albert Grammar School, both achieved away from home. With six wins, one loss, and two washouts from nine matches, the 1st XI team are equal first on the table heading into the winter break.

sport

yachting

The School’s Yachting team began their 2026 season with a fifth place finish at the Auckland Secondary Schools’ Fleet Racing Regatta, with captain Thomas Pilkington (pictured above, fifth from right) winning the ICLA Class race.

Following a disappointing result in 2025, the Premier team sailed extremely well at the New Zealand Secondary Schools’ Keelboat Championships to qualify first. However, the team failed to capitalise on previous momentum with unforced errors costing them valuable points, resulting in a second place finish by a single point. The School’s second team, with many of them being new to keelboat sailing, worked through some unfavourable conditions to finish ninth overall.

At the Auckland Regional Teams Racing Championships, both teams demonstrated exceptional teamwork and tactical understanding to finish first and second. At the New Zealand Teams’ Sailing Championships in the Term 1 holidays, the team went undefeated in qualifying, but some errors saw the team finish second overall behind eventual winners Westlake Boys’ High School. Their second placing does earn the team direct entry for the Interdominion Championships later this year.

The 2025 academic results were very pleasing, with senior students performing extremely well in all external assessments.

The Cambridge examination performances, particularly for A Level, were very strong. The percentage of A* and A grades were 41.4%, with the overall pass rate sitting at 98.8%. The AS results were also fantastic, with the A-C pass rate exceeding almost 69% and the overall pass rate at 95.8%, the most successful pass rate for the past eight years. This represents an exceptionally strong performance in one of the world’s most academically demanding secondary school qualifications.

The School’s NCEA cohort also performed very well. At NCEA Level 2, 69% of students gained their certificates, with 25 students gaining their certificates endorsed with Merit. At NCEA Level 3, 83% of students achieved the qualification, and 19 students had their certificates endorsed with Merit. Over 645 of NCEA Level 3 students achieved University Entrance in 2025.

Auckland Grammar School students study predominantly rigorous, academic subjects and standards which lead to comparatively high University Entrance rates for the Form 7 cohort. Cambridge students achieved a University Entrance pass rate of 99.6% in 2025 (which is up 0.3% from 2024), and the NCEA Form 7 cohort achieved 64.4% (up almost 5% year-on-year). Combined, the Form 7 students in 2025 achieved a University Entrance pass rate of 85.7%.

In 2025, the national pass rate for all students was 49.9% and the figure for all students in the School’s Equity Index (EQI) was 70.1%. The 2025 national average for boys in schools in the EQI achieving University Entrance was 62.5%. Across all schools in New Zealand, the University Entrance rate for boys sat at 43.4%.

Auckland Grammar School was once again one of the top three performing schools in the country in NZQA Scholarship examinations, after students earned 198 individual scholarship awards in 2025. These are students from the top 10% of the entire Form 7 cohort, plus Form 6 students from classes 6A and 6B.

an overview of the 2025 academic results

The list of students who earned Outstanding Cambridge Learner Awards for 2025 was released in February, and Auckland Grammar School earned a collective 15 High Achievement and Top in New Zealand Awards, as well as two Best Across AS and A Level Subject Awards.

Current Form 7 student Owen Li Zhang was named Top in New Zealand with Best Across 4 AS Level Subjects, and 2025 Dux Taejeong Kim was named Top in New Zealand with Best Across 3 A Level Subjects.

Six Top in New Zealand Awards were gained by Leo Briggs (A Level Design and Technology), Tony Chou (A Level English Literature), Maxwell Clarke (AS Level Physics), Harry Fan (AS Level Chemistry), current Form 7 student Toby King (ASLevel Economics) and Ao Ran Liu (AS Level Biology).

Seven High Achievement Awards were given to students who were Top in New Zealand but in subjects with limited entries: Harry Fan (AS Level Economics), Charles Fearon (AS Level Geography), Aaryan Maganlal (AS Level English), Scott Moore (AS Level Accounting), Xiao Shen (AS Level Business Studies), Aoxuan Yu (A Level Digital Media and Design) and Gary Yuan (A Level Spanish Language and Literature).

2025 outstanding cambridge learner awards

The B. F. Connell Prize for Dux of the School was awarded to Taejeong Kim, whose senior academic career has been defined by his exceptional capabilities and scholarly achievements. Originally from Korea, Taejeong started midway through Form 4 and finished at the top of 7A. His reports have consistently noted his meticulous work ethic and unwavering commitment to excellence, earning A grades for attitude and effort in each subject throughout the 2025 academic year.

In Form 6, Taejeong scored 75% in Cambridge AS Level English, 96% in each of his AS Levels in Biology, Chemistry and Physics, and 99% in AS Level Mathematics. He was also awarded Top in NZ in AS Biology and Best in NZ across 4 AS Levels for the November 2024 series.

Taejeong’s Form 7 academic record was extremely impressive. In the end-of-year A Level examinations, Taejeong secured an aggregate of 384/400, placing him first in Class in each of his four A Levels and also first in 7A. He scored marks of 93% in Biology, 95% in Chemistry, 96% in Physics and 100% in Maths. His teachers describe him as an intelligent, quietly determined student whose engagement with complex concepts is exemplary. He also received the Rabone Prize for Form 7 Science and the O’Sullivan Prize for Biology.

Taejeong embodies the School’s value of excellence, always seeking to perform at the highest possible standards. He is described by teachers as phenomenal and an outstanding student who is laser-focused on end goals. This year, he will be starting a Bachelor of Medical Science at Monash University in Melbourne.

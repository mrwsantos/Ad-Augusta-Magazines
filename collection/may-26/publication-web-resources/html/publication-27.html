Calling for Nominations

We are now accepting nominations for the annual Auckland Grammar School Old Boys’ Association Augusta Awards for 2026. Candidates will be considered for two types of awards - the Augusta Award and the Lion Award, introduced in 2018. These will be presented at the annual Augusta Awards Dinner on Thursday 5 November 2026.

Augusta Awards

The annual Augusta Awards evolved from the Association’s Old Boy of the Year Award. They are awarded to Old Boys who are at the top of their respective industries or specialties, or who have demonstrated a remarkable contribution to the Arts, Business, Sport and/or Public Service.

More specifically, they are intended to acknowledge Old Boys who have made a significant contribution in their chosen field, who have made a positive impact in their field throughout their careers and who have used their talents to encourage and inspire others to follow their lead.

The 2025 Augusta Awards were presented by former Headmaster John Morris, ONZM to Dr Murray MacCormick, CNZM ‘59, David Morgan, ONZM ‘73 and Richard Chambers ‘85.

Recipients of this award demonstrate leadership, integrity, excellence, respect, courage, pride, commitment, and humility, and their contributions have positively impacted others. Ideally, these Old Boys will have contributed or been connected in some way to Grammar.

Lion Award

This award seeks to acknowledge, in some way, younger Old Boys who, at an earlier stage in their career, are demonstrating leadership and/or a remarkable achievement for their age.

Specifically, this award is for Old Boys who demonstrate outstanding achievement, leadership, integrity, excellence, respect, courage, pride, commitment and humility, and are in early or mid-career.

Early achievement may include attaining a national sporting achievement, being a senior executive or director reaching a partnership in a notable professional services firm at an unusually young age or, equally, being acknowledged in Academia, the Arts, the Sciences and/or Technology fields.

The 2025 Lion Award was presented by former Headmaster John Morris, ONZM to Olympic Gold medallist Matt Macdonald.

If you know someone who you think is a suitable candidate for either award, please submit your nomination by 3 July 2026 via community.ags.school.nz. You can also email your nomination (with any attachments) to agsoba@ags.school.nz.

We look forward to announcing the results of the nominations later this year.

Join the community

All Old Boys are welcome to become members of the Old Boys’ Association. Stay connected with your peers and interact with other Old Boys who form a strong business and global social network.

There are two options available - an annual membership for $60 per year, or a one-off Life Membership for $695. To join, head to community.ags.school.nz and click on ‘Become a member’ under the Old Boys’ Association section.

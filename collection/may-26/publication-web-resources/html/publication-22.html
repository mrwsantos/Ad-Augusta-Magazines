2026 Art House Tour

Save the date for the sixth biennial tour - saturday 21 November 2026.

Above: Some of the houses featured on the fifth biennial Art House Tour in November 2024.

We are delighted to announce that the sixth Auckland Grammar School Foundation Trust Art House Tour will occur on Saturday 21 November 2026.

The Art House Tour originally began as an annual Art Expo, which was launched in 2002. In 2016, the Art Expo became the biennial Art House Tourto help establish a point of difference from the other art shows hosted by other Auckland schools.

The Art House Tour will feature a selection of beautiful homes in and around the Grammar community with artworks of over 100 established and emerging New Zealand artists, including Grammar masters Justin Boroughs, Tom Lusk and Gordon Pope, alongside Old Boys Brad Novak ‘89 and Logan Bow ‘17. The Old Boys’ Pavilion on campus will also host a gallery with pieces available to purchase and take home on the day.

All proceeds from the Art House Tour will go towards supporting the Auckland Grammar School Academic Endowment Fund, which allows the School to recruit, retain and reward exceptional teachers for our young men.

Tickets for this year’s Tour will go on sale to the Grammar community in August.

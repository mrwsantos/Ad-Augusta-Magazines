Volleyball

The Premier team competed at the New Zealand Secondary Schools’ Championships in Palmerston North. The team played Wellington High School in their first fixture, losing the first two sets (19-25 and 17-25 respectively), but winning the third time-affected set 18-16. In their second game against Christchurch Boys’ High School, the team won the first set 25-23. With a lapse in discipline, they then lost the second set 18-25. In the final time-affected set, they won 7-4.

On Day two, the team played Inglewood High School, winning 2-1. The second match against Tawa College saw the team win 2-0 (25-13, 25-23). Day three saw the Premier team lose 2-1 to Hutt International Boys’ School, before securing a 2-1 win against Rototuna High School. On day four, the team had two wins against Aotea College and Hutt Valley High School to win their division.

Water Polo

The Premier Water Polo team secured a top eight position at the North Island Secondary School Division Water Polo Championships by qualifying 2nd in pool play. The one loss in pool play was against Hamilton Boys’ High School (13-15).

In the cross over matches, Grammar drew with King’s College but lost to Sacred Heart College and Westlake Boys’ High School, meaning Grammar had to play off for the 5-8 positions.

Cricket

Competing at the Under 15 Junior Invitational Cricket Tournament, the Colts Cricket team played their first game against New Plymouth Boys’ High School, starting their tournament off with a six-wicket win. This was followed up by a five-wicket win over their opponents from Christchurch Boys’ High School. Day three saw the team’s game against Palmerston North Boys’ High School affected by rain, with the team winning using the Duckworth-Lewis Method, with the game on day four cancelled. The final day of play saw the team play St Paul’s Collegiate to determine the final placings. Dismissing the opposition for 114, the game came down to the final 10 overs. At 9 down, the Colts team ran a single to win the game and the tournament undefeated.

Golf

At the Auckland Stroke play Championships, Tony Zhang finished on 5 under par over the two days of competition to place second.

Rowing

The Rowing squad earned places in 10 A-finals over the course of the two days at the New Zealand Secondary Schools’ Championships at Lake Ruataniwha in Twizel. The Under 17 Coxed Eight crew clashed with St Peter’s College and St Kentigern College in a tight race for the podium, with the crew working hard to secure Bronze by half a second. The Under 17 Coxed Four also claimed Bronze amidst a tightly packed field. The Novice Under 18 Coxed Octuple Sculls crew won Silver, just one second behind the Gold medallist crew.

The final race of the regatta was the Under 18 Coxed Eight race for the Maadi Cup. The Senior Eight crew earned direct entry into the final after winning their heat. In the final, King’s College established early control and the Senior Eight rowed exceptionally well in a competitive final to earn Silver after a very tight final 250m,

where they were challenged by Hamilton Boys’ High School and St Bede’s College, who went on to claim Bronze.

Tennis

In their first pool stage match at the New Zealand Secondary Schools Championships, the Premier Tennis team played Christ’s College, securing a 4-2 win, making for a positive start to the tournament. Day two saw the team play St Kentigern College. A number of super tiebreak points challenged the team, but they prevailed with a 5-1 victory to progress straight through to the semi-finals. The team played their final pool match against St Andrew’s College, winning 4-2 to finish top of the pool. The penultimate day of play saw the team play in the semi-final against Christchurch Boys’ High School, winning 4-1.

The Premier Tennis team played St Kentigern College in the New Zealand Secondary Schools Championship Final. Multiple super tiebreak points were played, with Ridley Huang losing his singles match by a narrow 6-10 margin in a final set tiebreak. After four matches, the team were 3-1 ahead, with Captain of Tennis Luca Bland securing a straight sets victory (6-2, 6-1). This put the Premier team in a strong position, but they still needed to win one more match to secure the Championship for the second year in a row. In the doubles, Luca Bland teamed up with Yida Wu to win 6-4, 6-0. The overall result was a 4-2 win for the Premier Tennis team and a second consecutive National title; a reward for their determination and dedication throughout the season, and a fitting way to cap off a very successful year.

Triathlon

Unfortunately, the New Zealand Schools’ Triathlon Championships in Tauranga were cancelled due to extreme wind gusts. The organisers did organise an open-water swim in the sheltered harbour, which Auckland Grammar School students completed.

Yachting

The Yachting team competed in the Auckland Teams Championships, hosted at the Royal Akarana Yacht Club. The breeze was light, posing challenges for competing teams.

The School’s Gold team started the final day in third place and met Westlake Boys’ High School in the semi-final. At 1-1, the team won the final race to secure the win.

The School’s Blue team started the last day leading the competition and maintained their composure to finish the round-robin series in first place. They beat Takapuna Grammar School in the semi-final. Due to unfavourable conditions, the final could not be sailed, so the Blue team were named the Auckland champions, with the Gold team finishing second overall.

The team, led by captain Thomas Pilkington, crossed stage on the

penultimate day of Term 1 to receive their Auckland Championship medals and the School’s congratulations.

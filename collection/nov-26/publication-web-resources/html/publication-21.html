Where Creativity Comes Alive

events

tickets are now on sale for the sixth biennial art house tour.

The Auckland Grammar School Foundation Trust launched the annual Art Expo in 2002. In 2016, the Art Expo became the Art House Tour to help establish a point of difference from the other fundraising art shows hosted by other Auckland schools.

2026 marks the sixth biennial Art House Tour, and tickets are now on sale for this one-day event, taking place on Saturday 21 November. The Tour offers attendees the opportunity to take in seven beautiful homes in and around the Grammar community whilst viewing an extensive range of curated artwork. The experience is set to delight with over 70 artists (including Old Boys Brad Novak ‘89 and Logan Bow ‘17) exhibiting alongside Auckland Grammar School’s emerging young artists.

Each of the seven houses on the Tour has a story to tell, including an historic heritage property in Epsom, two 1920s homes in

Remuera, and an award-winning home in Herne Bay. In addition to each of the homes, the Gallery located in the Old Boys’ Pavilion on campus will also display works of art for sale. This will be open to both Tour attendees and the general public on Friday 20 and Saturday 21 November.

All proceeds from the Tour go towards supporting the Academic Endowment Fund, which allows Auckland Grammar School to recruit, retain and reward exceptional teachers for our young men in the classroom. Tickets for the Tour are $110 per person and can be purchased via the Art House Tour website (arthousetour.co.nz). Enjoy a fabulous day out with friends and family - we look forward to seeing you there.

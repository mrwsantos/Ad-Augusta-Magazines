Almost two years ago, Auckland Grammar School decided to pursue the purchase of 10 Clive Road to become the cornerstone of a new Grammar Precinct, with the community at its heart. The purchase of this property will enable improvements to Tibbs House for the boarders and house masters who call it home. It also provides the possibility to expand staff housing within a much-enjoyed community surrounding the School.

In December 2024, knowing how valuable the 2,000m² property would be to Grammar, the owner offered it to the School first to see if there was interest in purchasing it, before taking it to the open market. The answer was a resounding ‘thank you for the opportunity, we would love to see if we can make this work.’

Neighbouring 8 Clive Road, at 1,000m² is already in Grammar’s possession. Beside No. 8, Tibbs House sits on a large site on the corner of Clive and Mountain Roads. Consequently, securing No. 10 creates a contiguous landholding, which Headmaster Tim O’Connor says is key. “10 Clive Road is a strategic purchase for the School. It is a multidimensional, legacy piece of land where location is everything. When the three properties are viewed collectively it unlocks all sorts of possibilities for us as a school community.”

The three sites offer considerable potential but realising that vision requires ownership of No. 10 by a trust entity at Auckland Grammar School by December 2026. And to own it, requires funds.

Funding the purchase

The agreed purchase price two years ago was under market value at just over $4.4 million. A generous Old Boy paid the $1.5 million deposit, which he has kindly since gifted to the School. Another Old Boy offered to pay the balance of the price to secure the property himself in the interim, so that the owner could move out and move on with her life. The generosity of these two men ensured the property did not go to the open market but would later be able to go into Grammar’s possession.

For the past two years, Auckland Grammar School has successfully fundraised towards the agreed milestones to pay back $2.9 million to the temporary owner of the house. If the entire amount can be paid back in full by the end of 2026, the property will be in the School’s possession.

The School’s Director of Advancement Jeff Blackburn ‘67 says the wider Grammar community has understood “that this is an opportunity that simply cannot be missed. As such, they collectively helped us pay back $1 million to the interim owner

at the end of 2025.” The remaining $1.9 million needed to be raised by the end of 2026 has been going well, with $565,000 remaining to be found as at the end of September 2026. However, Jeff notes that the clock is ticking. “We now only have a couple of months to source that support, but we are hopeful our community will help make the purchase of 10 Clive Road a reality for Auckland Grammar School.”

The School community has always managed to fund vital things into reality throughout its history. From the World War I obelisk in 1922 through to the Te Ara library complex and the new pool in 2024. Tim says the hallmark of the Grammar community over time has been that “we take control of our own destiny through the active engagement of our community, feeding forward to provide us with the Auckland Grammar School we have today.”

Tim muses on a Greek proverb: A society grows great when old men plant trees in whose shade they shall never sit. “This proverb accurately reflects our community’s way of supporting a Grammar education through time. It is now our turn as we look ahead to provide future students, staff, headmasters and boards with the legacy purchase of 10 Clive Road, and the enormous amenity that will bring.”

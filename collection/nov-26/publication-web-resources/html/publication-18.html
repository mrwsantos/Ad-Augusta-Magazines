A highlight of the School’s calendar, the 21st annual Foundation Trust Gala Dinner, in association with Barfoot & Thompson, took place on Saturday 23 May 2026 with 360 guests enjoying a wonderful evening ina beautiful setting in the Great Hall.

With performances from the Kapa Haka group, led by Prefect Nikau-Te Ha Thorpe, and country singer Tami Neilson backed by Grammarphonics, the event was MC’d by Old Boy Scotty Stevenson ‘90 for the fifth consecutive year. The evening helped to raise over $320,000 for the School’s Academic Endowment Fund.

This year’s Gala Dinner Committee was chaired by current parent Sophie Anderson in her first year in the role. The School wishes to thank everyone who helped to make the Gala Dinner a fun and hugely successful evening.

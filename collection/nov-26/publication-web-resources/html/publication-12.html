NOVEMBER 2026

Auckland Grammar School has had a busy and successful 2026 Rugby season. A total of 14 teams were represented across different age groups and weight grades, with four teams qualifying for their respective finals.

The 1st XV team had a phenomenal season, finishing with a 17 win, one loss record. They played an exciting and impressive brand of Rugby throughout the season, which saw them progress to the final held at Eden Park against Kelston Boys’ High School. The team gave their all throughout the game despite an unfortunate 15-27 loss.

The Rugby grade finals were held in August, with the 6A and 7A teams winning their games to be crowned champions. 6A had a fantastic season, training hard and playing consistently, winning their final 53-5 against King’s College. 4B played spirited, gritty Rugby and was up for it in the final against an in-form Sacred Heart College. Unfortunately, the opposition proved too good and managed to come away with a 19-10 win. 7A trained with dedication and consistency, and had to dig deep for most of their results. They finished in the top two teams, setting up a final against Howick College. Their final was played in incredibly tough conditions, and they played with incredible heart and grit to come out victorious against a strong and ever-improving Howick team 30-0. That makes it seven years in a row for 7A, which is a remarkable achievement and a new record.

Congratulations to the following students who have been named in Auckland Rugby representative teams - Kelekolio Asi, Sol Anderson, Louie Du Plessis, Ashton Kirke, Connor Murdoch, Bobby Neels, Nico Stanley, Luke Thomson and Matayas Thomson made the Auckland Under 18 team, while Ethan Halaapiapi and Ben Stewart made the Auckland Under 18 Gulls side. In late September, Nico was also selected for the New Zealand Schools team, and Sol Anderson was selected for the New Zealand Māori Under 18 squad. We congratulate these young men on their achievements.

The 2026 season for the 1st XI Hockey team, captained by Form 7 student Joel Sansom, was one of steady growth, resilience and a number of memorable performances.

Traditional fixtures remained an important part of the season, including a 1-1 draw against Tauranga Boys’ College. A major highlight of the season came in the traditional fixture against Whangārei Boys’ High School, where the 1st XI claimed a commanding 5-2 victory to secure the Shay Neal/Gus Lindsay Shield (pictured above).

In the regular College Sport competition, the 1st XI team produced victories over Sacred Heart College, Pakuranga College, Rangitoto College, St Kentigern College and Mount Roskill Grammar School.

The team remained competitive throughout the season, showing attacking quality, determination and strong team spirit. A 2-2 draw with St Kentigern set up a final playoff, in which the 1st XI team narrowly lost 3-2, finishing fourth in the Auckland competition.

The team look to the New Zealand Secondary Schools Championships in Dunedin (also known as the Rankin Cup), where they will aim to finish the 2026 season strongly on the national stage.

The School’s Skiing team competed at the annual Auckland Secondary Schools Skiing Championships at Snowplanet in June. The competition included 90 students from across the region.

In the Ski Race, held over two runs, Form 6 student Nicholas Wu was fifth with a combined time of 31.28 seconds, with Form 7 student Tom Pilkington in seventh place with a time of 31.86 seconds. Not too far behind were Form 6 student Samuel Mace in 32.77 seconds and Form 7 student George Turner with 33.36 seconds. In the Slopestyle competition, Form 7 student Cameron Symonds was third in the seniors.

In the Ski Dual Championship - a knock-out competition - Form 6 student Nicholas Wu was third, with classmate Liam Zeng taking fourth place in the Dual Plate final. The team placed third overall in Auckland, with several junior students performing well.

squash

The Premier Squash team, captained by Campbell Dunwoodie (back row, centre).

In early August, the Premier Squash team competed in the National Secondary Schools Squash Championships in Christchurch, with 54 teams competing, including 31 teams in the boys’ draw competing across two separate divisions.

The Premier team, captained by Prefect Campbell Dunwoodie, began the tournament with a bye in the first round of matches, followed by 4-1 wins over both Palmerston North Boys’ High School and Wellington College. The fourth round and semi-final was against third-seed, Christchurch Boys’ High School. The Premier team rose to the occasion, securing a resounding 4-1 victory.

The final was against top-seeded Tauranga Boys College. Their team had a significant advantage in squash grading points, with three of the four students representing New Zealand at the World Junior Squash Championships in July. Tauranga Boys’ College had not conceded a single game at the tournament and, despite a determined effort, the Premier team lost 0-5 in the final, earning second place and the National Secondary Schools’ Silver medals.

Congratulations are extended to Thevan Gunawardena who was named as a non-travelling reserve in the New Zealand Secondary Schools’ Junior Squash Team at the prizegiving ceremony. Special thanks go to the team’s manager, Simon Lassche, for his ongoing support and guidance of the students.

The School’s Cycling squad has delivered an exceptional campaign across road and track racing.

In the College Sport Secondary Schools Team Time Trial series, the Senior A team captured the WH Bigwood Memorial Trophy with a victory on Finals Day, while the Junior A squad earned third place overall.

The team also performed strongly in the Points Race, highlighted by gold-medal performances from Heston Lubeck in the Under 14 race and Levi Mitchell in the Under 17 division, with Thomas Still finishing second.

At the North Island Secondary Schools Road Championships, Auckland Grammar School placed second overall for boys’ secondary schools. The Senior A Team Time Trial squad was second, while Heston Lubeck won the Under 14 Road Race and placed second in the Criterium.

Oliver Patterson (Under 15 Road Race) and James Fearon (Under 17 Road Race and Criterium) both added second-place finishes, supported by Louie Ulmer’s third-place finish in the Under 16 Road Race.

At the Track Cycling Championships, Oliver Patterson and James Fearon secured silver medals in the Under 15 and Under 17 Individual Omniums, respectively, with James winning both the Scratch and Points races. The momentum continued in the team events, where the Senior A squad won gold in the Under 20 Team Pursuit, and the Junior A squad placed third in both the Team Pursuit and Team Sprint.

Overall, it has been a successful season for the Cycling squad. At the time of printing, the team was preparing for the New Zealand Secondary Schools Road Championships just prior to the end of Term 3.

During the Term 2 holidays, the School’s Orienteering team competed at the New Zealand Secondary Schools Orienteering Championships.

The Senior Relay team finished in fourth place, while Form 6 student Isaac Stewart placed second in the Long event and was the winner of the Sprint race held at Lincoln University. This earned him the title of National Champion in the Senior Boys’ Sprint.

On the final day of competition, the three-hour Rogaine race saw Isaac team up with Prefect Lachlan Cameron, using the hills to their advantage to place second with the Junior team of Tristan Jayasuriya, Frederik McGregor-MacDonald and Shuhan Xu finishing in fourth place. The strong results from the team over the course of the races saw the Orienteering team earn third place in the Premier Boys competition.

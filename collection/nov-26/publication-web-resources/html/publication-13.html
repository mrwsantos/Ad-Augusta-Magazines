sport

football

After losing the core of the team after the 2025 season, a number of new players earned selection to the 1st XI Football team for the 2026 season, which was exciting for the programme. However, the 1st XI had a mixed season.

The team unfortunately lost all four traditional fixtures against Tauranga Boys’ College, New Plymouth Boys’ High School, Hamilton Boys’ High School, and King’s College. The loss to King’s College at the end of the season meant the 1st XI relinquished their grasp of the Morris-Kelley Cup for the first time since 2019, which they are already eager to win back in 2027.

The Auckland Premier League saw the 1st XI team produce some more positive performances and results. They ended the season with a five win, seven loss, and two draw record to place fifth in the competition. The highlight of the season was the team’s 2-1 away win over Westlake Boys’ High School. This match ultimately set the tone and direction for the 1st XI team’s successful National Tournament campaign just a few weeks later.

This year the 1st XI unfortunately did not make a ‘Cup run’, as they drew neighbours St Peter’s College in an unusual preliminary round. Despite controlling long periods of the match with superior ball control, the team failed to create enough clear-cut chances, and instead conceded goals from errors to lose 0-3, which meant the team progressed no further in this competition.

swimming

In July, 15 students represented the School at the New Zealand Secondary Schools Swimming Championships. Many swimmers set new personal bests in their individual events, while relay teams continued to secure five medals.

Form 7 student Yen-Cheng Lee achieved a personal best time of 1:07.88 in the 100m Breaststroke final, with Form 6 student Daniel Ecclestone securing Gold in the 100m Butterfly in a personal best time of 55.73 seconds.

In the 200m Freestyle, Form 5 student Tyler Lushkott sprinted back from sixth place to third in the last 50m, earning a well-deserved Bronze medal. He also won the Intermediate National title for the 400m Freestyle.

The Senior 4x50m Relay team displayed real grit to earn Bronze, while momentum continued in the 8x50m relay race with the team earning the Silver medal, beating Hamilton Boys’ High School by just 0.21 seconds. The greatest achievement of the tournament was winning the National title of Top Boys’ School, demonstrating the team’s strength throughout the competition.

The winners of the Junior Interschool Competition, AGS 3.

The Premier Basketball team began their 2026 season with three convincing wins in their traditional fixtures against Hamilton Boys’ High School, New Plymouth Boys’ High School and Tauranga Boys’ College.

In the Auckland Premiership, the team won their semi-final against Dilworth School, before progressing to the final against Westlake Boys’ High School. It was an evenly matched affair, ending in a 95-107 defeat. The team also qualified for the National Tournament after coming second in the AA Zone 1 Regional Qualifiers, losing only to Westlake again in the final, 84-96.

The Under 17A team were second in the Auckland Secondary Schools’ Central West Zone Competition, losing 40-47 to Mount Albert Grammar School in the final. They went on to win the Greater Auckland Championships for the fourth consecutive year. The Under 17B Team won their Auckland Secondary Schools’ Central West Zone Competition, winning77-45 against Mount Albert Grammar School.

The Junior A1 Team were undefeated in their Auckland Secondary Schools’ Central West Zone Competition, winning 65-52 in the final against Liston College. They then went on to win the Greater Auckland Championships, with a 40-33 victory over Dilworth School. The Junior A2 team were fourth in their competition with a nine win, three loss record, including a nail-biting 62-60 win over Western Springs College in their final game.

This year also saw the introduction of the Junior Interschool Basketball Competition, with Grammar competing against St. Peter’s College. The season included a round-robin, as well as semi-finals and finals, from which AGS 3 emerged victorious.

A total of 28 teams competed in the Social Basketball League this year. The grand final against Skibidis and the Teachers saw Skibidis come away with the win.

winter tournament week

Above left: Form 6 student Finlay Duncan during the New Zealand Secondary Schools Weightlifting Championships. Above middle: Captain of Fencing Oscar Ardern congratulates his opponent. Above right: The 1st XI Football team finished eighth at their national tournament in Palmerston North.

At the New Zealand Secondary Schools Badminton Tournament in Porirua, the Premier Badminton team had a superb week, remaining undefeated through pool play. In the final against Wellington College, the teams won three matches each, so the result was decided by the total number of points, which the team won to earn the Division 2 National Title.

At the Regional Invitational Tournament held in Hawera, the Junior Basketball team lost 78-83 to Westlake Boys’ High School in the final to finish second overall.

The Premier Basketball team performed well, finishing second at the Regional Qualifying Tournament in Auckland. The team beat St Peter’s College in the semi-final, but unfortunately lost 84-96 to Westlake Boys’ High School in the final. This means they are seeded fourth and qualified for the National Secondary School Championships which took place in the first week of the Term 3 holidays in Palmerston North.

During the annual Northern Tour which was held at the end of Winter Tournament Week, the Cycling team earned a total of 13 medals across the three days of competition.

The Sabre Fencing team, captained by Form 6 student Oscar Ardern, competed at the New Zealand Secondary Schools Sabre Championships in Auckland. In the Men’s Individual Sabre, Form 6 student Isaac Yim battled a large pool of 29 to make the semi-final, where he fell to one of the top-seeded players in New Zealand, winning a joint Bronze podium finish. The Sabre team beat a combined team from the Hawkes Bay 45-13 in the semi-final, and, in the final, beat St Kentigern College by four points to secure the Gold medal and the National Title.

The 1st XI Football team placed eighth at the New Zealand Secondary Schools Championships in Palmerston North. The team unfortunately lost their quarter-final 1-3 to defending champions and eventual finalists, Scots College. In their playoff matches, the 1st XI team lost to Wellington College in a penalty shootout after

being 1-1 at the end of ordinary time and then lost 0-3 to New Plymouth Boys’ High School.

The New Zealand Secondary Schools Golf Finals were played at the start of Winter Tournament Week. The Grammar team finished 11th.

The 1st XI Hockey team placed 11th at the New Zealand Secondary Schools Tournament (known as the Rankin Cup) held in Dunedin. In their final two games, the team lost 3-4 to Hastings Boys’ High School, then went on to beat St Andrew’s College on shootouts, after scores were locked at 1-1 at the end of ordinary time.

The Under 15 Rugby team placed sixth at the Under 15 Invitational National Tournament held in Rotorua. The team lost 7-12 in a closely contested match against Napier Boys’ High School in the quarter-final. They beat Palmerston North Boys’ High School 19-10, and their final game was against Christchurch Boys’ High School, which they lost 26-41.

The Premier Table Tennis team was unbeaten throughout the New Zealand Secondary Schools Championships, dropping only one set. In the round robin section, the team beat a Palmerston North composite team, Havelock North High School and Hobsonville Point Secondary School. In the quarter-finals, the team beat third-seeded Westlake Boys’ High School and in the semi-finals, they beat Macleans College. The final played out according to seedings, with the team meeting the second seeds, St Kentigern College, whom they beat 4-0 to secure the National Title for the fifth consecutive year. In the Individual Under 19 competition, captain Hao Jiang met teammate Victor Li in the final, with Jiang winning Gold and Li Silver.

At the New Zealand Secondary Schools Weightlifting Championships, the team of six weightlifters registered personal bests, with four students getting to the podium, including Form 5 student Severyn Yushchenko who won Silver in the 95kg class. Severyn has only been in the sport for a term, and his total of 183kgs over the snatch and the clean and jerk would have been enough to win Gold in the 110kg weight class.
